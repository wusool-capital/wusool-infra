# Glossary

Terms used across this guide, in plain words.

## Deals and the CRM

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Source of record** | The system whose copy counts. For Wusool, that is Attio. |
| **Organization** | A company in Attio. One organization can be a buyer, a seller, or both. |
| **Role** | A buyer or seller profile attached to an organization. |
| **Vertical** | The sector a buyer role targets. An organization can hold one buyer role per vertical. |
| **Ticket size** | How much a buyer is willing to invest in one deal. |
| **Qualified Buy-side deal** | The Attio deal created when you approve a Toolkit match. |
| **Inbound deal** | A deal at its earliest stage, before anyone has qualified it. |
| **Promote** | Move an existing Inbound deal to Qualified, instead of creating a second deal. |

## Wusool Toolkit

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Fit score** | How well a seller matches the buyer's criteria. |
| **Data confidence** | How much of the fit score rests on real CRM data rather than AI guesses. A high fit score with low confidence needs checking. |
| **Enrich** | Let the bot research public sources and propose values for empty fields. |
| **Website check** | A message asking you to confirm a Google Maps lead whose website didn't match. |

## WusoolScribe

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **System audio** | The sound your Mac plays, such as the other people on a call. |
| **Transcription model** | The speech-to-text engine Scribe downloads and runs on your Mac. |
| **Summary provider** | The AI service that writes the summary. Ollama runs on your Mac; the others are online services. |
| **Push** | Send a finished meeting to Attio as a note. |
| **Scribe Push** | The Settings tab holding the server address and key your administrator gives you. |
| **Install ID** | A code identifying your copy of Scribe. Support asks for it instead of your key. |

## Website and Sanity

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Gated report** | A report that shows its first page, then asks the reader to confirm their email to read the rest. |
| **Slug** | The last part of a page's web address, for example `gcc-healthcare` in `/reports/gcc-healthcare`. |
| **Silo** | The website topic a report or article is filed under, such as Business Valuation or Exit Strategy. |
| **Pin** | Feature a report at the top of `/reports` or in the home page banner. |
| **Publish / unpublish** | Make a Sanity document live on the website, or take it down. |

## n8n

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Workflow** | An automation made of connected steps. |
| **Node** | One step in a workflow. |
| **Execution** | One run of a workflow, with each node's input and output. |
| **Environment** | Development (for testing) or production (live). They are separate. |
