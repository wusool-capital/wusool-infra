# November 2026

Delivery period: 15 October – 17 November 2026.

In five weeks, more than 110 pull requests were merged across every Wusool
product. Three new surfaces launched: the Wusool Engine, the Wusool MCP for
Claude, and a web version of find-match. A new interactions engine now keeps
Attio's relationship fields current from email and calendar. WusoolScribe
went from 0.7.7 to 0.8.7.

## Highlights

- **The Wusool Engine.** A web app where advisors browse, filter, and edit
  buyers and sellers on live data. It is live at `engine.wusoolcapital.com`.
- **Wusool MCP for Claude.** Advisors connect Claude once and can find, add,
  edit, and bulk-import buyers and sellers by chatting.
- **Find-match on the web.** The full find-match run now has a web page at
  `/app`, with a ranked shortlist, reasoning, and in-page seller research.
- **Bulk upload.** `/bulk-buyer` and `/bulk-seller` add many profiles from
  one CSV or Excel file, checked in full before anything is saved.
- **Relationship tracking in Attio.** Last interaction, next interaction, and
  connection strength now update on Organizations, People, and Deals.
- **A simpler deal pipeline.** Deals now have 10 stages and a separate status.
- **Interactive reports.** Reports and Insights articles can keep their
  charts and clicks. Whole-page HTML articles are supported.
- **Website analytics.** PostHog now shows traffic, tool funnels, leads,
  bookings, and report reads on one dashboard.
- **WusoolScribe 0.8.7.** Speaker names, scannable summaries, proofreading,
  and meeting notes with a floating notepad.
