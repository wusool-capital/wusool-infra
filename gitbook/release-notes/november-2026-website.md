# November 2026: Website lead tools and reports

Delivery period: 15 October – 17 November 2026.

## Interactive reports and Insights

- **Keep it interactive.** A report with this option opens as the live
  report after the form. The free preview stays flat.
- **Live charts in articles.** An Insights article shows still images for
  its charts. They turn live as the reader scrolls to them.
- **Whole-page articles.** An Insights article can be an exported HTML file
  shown as the whole page, with its own design, sidebar, and charts. The
  text still goes to Webflow for search engines. **Hide header and author
  details** leaves only the site navbar.
- **Larger reports save.** A report's pictures are now stored as images, so
  big reports no longer hit a size limit.
- **HTML upload.** A report's HTML can be uploaded as a file instead of
  pasted.
- **Banner link wording.** The home page banner's link text is editable per
  report, under **Banner button text**. No Webflow edit is needed.
- **Credit Wusool Capital.** An Insights article can name Wusool Capital as
  its author, with the brand mark. The Team page does not list it.

## Publish with Claude

- **A Claude skill for staff.** It creates and publishes reports and
  Insights articles from a normal Claude chat. It checks the content
  against what the site renders and publishes only on approval.
- **Worth publishing?** Before a draft, it scores whether the piece suits
  a blog article and checks for overlap with existing ones.
- **Plain words.** It talks to staff without field names or jargon.

## Reports on phones and in Safari

- **Fit any screen.** Fixed-size pages now shrink to the screen width.
  Readers can pinch to zoom.
- **No cut-off pages on iPhone.** Safari no longer enlarges text past the
  page edge, and full-page images show whole.
- **Links work.** Links in a report open in a new tab. Contents links scroll
  to their section instead of loading the site inside the report.
- **Report PDFs load once.** See the fix under
  [Platform](november-2026-platform.md).

## Lead emails

- **Replies start in a draft.** Replying to a lead notice opens a draft
  addressed to the lead. It thanks them, asks for a short call, and links
  the booking calendar. The team inbox is copied, so everyone sees the
  thread.
- **Clearer notices.** The notice lists every form field and links the
  lead's email. Seller and buyer notices have their own titles, such as
  "New Seller Lead".
- **A bad email address no longer breaks the run.** The team still gets the
  notice, with a warning in the subject.
- **Notices also reach the tech mailbox.** This covers all five lead tools.
- **Report code emails reach inboxes.** They are sent from a dedicated
  address, include a plain-text version, and show "Wusool Capital" as the
  sender name.
- **Book a Call tracking.** The button goes through the Toolkit so clicks
  are counted. A lead's first campaign tags are saved with the lead.
- **Privacy Policy checkbox** on the report form, saved with the lead.

## Website analytics

- **PostHog dashboard.** A Website Metrics dashboard shows traffic, tool
  funnels, leads, bookings, report reads, and consent choices.
- **Consent first.** Page views, time on page, and tool and report events
  are sent only after a visitor chooses **Accept all**. A visitor who
  declined is counted without a name or email.
- **Session replay.** For visitors who accept, replay shows how the tools
  and reports are used. Name, email, phone, and LinkedIn fields are hidden.
- **Button clicks.** Clicks on Get Started and Buyers are counted by button
  name only.
- **Acceptance rate.** Clicks on the consent banner's Accept and Decline are
  counted without identifying anyone.
