# November 2026: WusoolScribe

Delivery period: 15 October – 18 November 2026. WusoolScribe went from
version 0.7.7 to 0.8.7. Every version is listed in the
[Scribe changelog](scribe-changelog.md).

## Who said what

- **Speaker names on every line.** Your lines carry your name. Scribe
  separates the other voices on your Mac after the meeting.
- **Voices are remembered.** Name a person once and Scribe recognizes them
  in later meetings. Voice fingerprints stay on your Mac.
- **Pushed meetings name speakers.** Attio notes now show names instead of
  "Participant".
- **Who Spoke?** Merge two voices that are one person, separate again from
  scratch, and play each voice's sample to check who it is.
- **Change Speaker.** Move transcript lines to another voice, to you, or to a
  speaker the separation missed.
- **Imported audio works too.** Scribe separates speakers in imported files.
- **Safer auto-naming.** A voice is named only on a clear match and is
  marked for you to confirm.
- **Faster and lighter.** Separation runs faster by default, shows time left
  and progress, and is stopped and retried if it runs far too long.
  **Separate Speakers Automatically** can be turned off.

## Better summaries

- **Gist first.** A summary opens with a 2–3 sentence gist. Next steps,
  decisions, and notes by topic follow. Buyer, seller, and investor
  meetings add a deal signal.
- **Notes grow with the meeting** instead of stopping at a fixed length.
- **Dates make sense.** "This weekend" becomes a real date, worked out from
  the meeting day.
- **Proofread you can trust.** Proofread knows the meeting's speakers,
  title, and your own vocabulary, plus common deal and finance terms. It no
  longer renames people to look-alike words. It reports lines it couldn't
  check.
- The same new order shows in Scribe, in the Attio note, and in matching.

## Meeting notes (0.8.7)

- **Notes beside the transcript.** While you record, type notes next to the
  transcript. Drag the divider to resize.
- **Floating notepad.** It stays on top of other apps, such as a
  full-screen call, and is hidden from screen shares.
- **Notes shape the summary.** The summary gives weight to what you noted.
  The Attio note shows them under **Advisor notes**.
- **Editable after a push.** Changes update the Attio note.

## Fixes

- Voice samples play on the current output device after headphones
  disconnect.
- Speaker names save when you click away from the field.
- Scribe opens in a larger window, and **Summarize** stays visible.
