# October 2026: WusoolScribe

Delivery period: 15 September – 10 October 2026. WusoolScribe went from
version 0.4.9 to 0.7.7. Every version is listed in the
[Scribe changelog](scribe-changelog.md).

## Recording

- **Auto-stop.** Scribe stops recording when a meeting ends. A 15-second
  countdown offers **Keep** and **Stop now**, and a setting turns it off.
- **Straight to review.** Stopping a recording opens the meeting as soon as
  it's saved, ready to edit or summarize.
- **Always ready.** **Open at Login** starts Scribe quietly in the menu bar,
  so it's already running when a meeting begins.
- **Recording indicator.** The menu-bar icon shows a red dot while recording
  and an amber dot when paused.
- **Smoother popups.** Meeting popups share a new design, behave like system
  notifications, and no longer steal focus from the meeting app.
- Start buttons react instantly, with a spinner while recording starts.

## Transcript editing

- **Line editing.** Tick lines, Shift-click for a range, and delete, merge,
  or split them before summarizing.
- **One-click clean-up.** Right-click a line to delete everything before it,
  clearing small talk in one step.
- **Find & replace** fixes a misheard name across the whole transcript.
- **Undo and redo** cover every change, and deleted lines are left out of
  the summary.
- A server endpoint for AI spelling corrections is in place, ready for the
  upcoming "Proofread" button.

## Organizing and deleting meetings

- **Delete meetings and folders,** individually, several at once, or a whole
  folder from the sidebar.
- **Remove from Attio too.** An opt-in box also removes pushed meetings from
  the server and Attio.
- **Folders.** They are ordered by recent activity, with a back button from
  a meeting to its folder.
- **Resizable sidebar.** Drag it up to 70% of the window; Scribe remembers
  the width.
- Fixes stop selections carrying between folders, which could have deleted
  the wrong meetings.

## Feedback and updates

- **In-app feedback.** A bug icon opens a form that reaches the team by email.
  Every submission is stored on the server, so none are lost.
- **Changelog.** A changelog page in these docs, linked from the menu bar and
  the update dialog.
- **New look.** The Inter font to match the Wusool Capital website, and the
  hand cursor on every clickable control.
