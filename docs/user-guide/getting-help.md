# Getting help

## Wusool Toolkit (Slack bot)

Contact the engineering owner with the command you ran, the organization
name, and a screenshot of the bot's reply.

## WusoolScribe

Contact the engineering owner with your Install ID (shown in
**Settings → Push Destination**), what you were doing, and a screenshot if
the app showed an error.

## Other tools

- **n8n** is the workflow-automation platform used internally by the team.
  It has its own login and its own [documentation](https://docs.n8n.io/).
- **Attio** is the CRM of record for the business team. The Toolkit bot and
  WusoolScribe both write into it; it is not managed from this
  documentation.
