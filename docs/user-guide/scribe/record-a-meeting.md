# Recording a meeting

1. Open WusoolScribe and select your microphone and system-audio device (see
   [Audio setup](audio-setup.md) if this is your first time).
2. Optionally name the meeting.
3. Press **Record**. WusoolScribe captures your microphone and your
   system's audio (for example, the other side of a video call) at the same
   time.
4. Pause or stop the recording at any point. Stopping ends the meeting and
   moves it to your meeting list.

![The recording controls: pause, stop, and a live audio level meter.](../../images/scribe-recording-controls.png)

All audio is saved locally on your computer. See
[Audio setup](audio-setup.md) for how microphone and system audio are
combined.
