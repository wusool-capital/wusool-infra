# Live transcription

While a meeting is recording, WusoolScribe transcribes speech as it happens
using a local speech-to-text model (Whisper or Parakeet). Nothing is sent to
the cloud for this step.

![A meeting's transcript building in real time while recording.](../../images/scribe-live-transcript.png)

- Each line is timestamped.
- You can copy the transcript at any time using the **Copy** button.
- You can change the transcription model or language from the toolbar
  (**Model**, **Language**) before or between recordings.
- You can choose your input/output devices from the toolbar (**Devices**).

Transcripts are stored locally alongside the meeting's recording and are
available from your meeting list after the meeting ends.
