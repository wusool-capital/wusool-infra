# AI summaries

Once a meeting has a transcript, WusoolScribe can generate a summary using
an AI provider of your choice:

- **Ollama** (runs locally on your machine — recommended for full privacy)
- **Claude**, **Groq**, or **OpenRouter**
- Any **custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint** your organization runs

Choose your provider once in **Settings**; it's used for every summary after
that.

The summary is editable — treat it as a first draft. Once you're happy with
it, you can [push it to the CRM](push-to-crm.md).
